  • Each power line represents a + / − pair; both splits happen at labeled screw-terminal bus strips (24 V strips on the converter's input side, 5 V strips on its output side), and all grounds are common at those strips.
  • The fuse block and DC wattmeter sit on the 24 V side; the LC filter and LED voltmeter clean and watch the 5 V rail on the mid-pole power board.
  • ⚠️ The Teensy 4.0's pins are 3.3 V logic — not 5 V tolerant; the LS7366R board runs at 3.3 V.
  • Left wheel = Sabertooth M1 (bytes 1–127) · right wheel = M2 (bytes 128–255), Simplified Serial at 9600 baud on Serial2.
  • SS1 / SS2 chip-selects address LS7366R #1 (left) and #2 (right) on the shared SPI bus.
